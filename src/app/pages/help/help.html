Bank accounts

How do I connect a bank?

Open the Accounts tab, choose Connect an account, and follow the secure Plaid Link steps. Choose your institution and finish its sign-in flow there. You can connect another institution from Accounts later.

Why are my transactions missing or still loading?

New connections can take a little time to prepare. Recent transactions usually appear first; Plaid may take a few minutes to finish importing older history. Check the sync message on Accounts or Details, then refresh the page. Daily account sync must be on for automatic background updates.

Why does a bank balance not match my bank's app?

Money Man displays the latest balance saved from Plaid, which may be cached and not real-time. Open Accounts and choose Refresh balances to request an account refresh. Pending activity and the timing of your bank's updates can also cause differences.

Can I rename a bank or an account, or leave one out of my totals?

Yes. On Accounts, choose Rename beside a bank or an account to give it a name you recognize, and leave the name blank to go back to the bank's own. Turn off Include in totals on an account to leave its transactions out of your spending totals, goals, and chat without disconnecting it. Turn it back on any time.

What happens when I disconnect a bank?

Disconnecting removes the saved bank connection and account records. Transactions already imported remain in your history, and manual transactions are not removed. To learn more about stored data, see Privacy & data.

Budgets and categories

Where do I set my monthly budget?

In Settings, open Budget & income, enter your monthly income and budget, then choose Save. Your budget should not exceed your income. Clearing the budget leaves your spending visible without a monthly budget target.

How do I set a limit for a category?

On Details, choose Edit categories to update limits together, or tap the pencil beside a category to change just that one. You can also open a category and choose Set a limit. Changing your monthly budget scales existing limits proportionally; saving the same budget leaves limits unchanged.

Why did I get a budget reminder?

A reminder appears when a category reaches 90% of its limit. Turn off Budget reminders in Settings to stop these reminders.

Transactions

How do I look at a different month?

On Details, use the month picker at the top. Choose any of the last 24 months, or use the arrows to step back and forward. Back to this month returns you to the current one. See every transaction keeps the month you are viewing.

How do I fix a transaction's category?

On Details, tap the transaction to open it, then choose a category. For a bank transaction, leave Also apply to every ... transaction ticked if you want matching transactions, including future ones, to use that category too. The thin colored bar on the left of each row shows its category. Your own category choice takes priority over automatic categorization.

Can I add a cash purchase or edit a transaction?

On Details, choose Add transaction next to the All transactions heading to enter one manually. Manual entries can be edited or deleted after you tap them open. Bank-imported transactions cannot be edited or deleted there, but you can change their category. Manual entries require a positive amount and a date within the supported 24-month history.

Why does it say "No spending yet"?

A new account has no transactions, so Home and Details show a prompt instead of numbers. Connect a bank on the Accounts tab or add your first transaction to see your own spending.

Goals

What kinds of goals can I add?

On Details, choose Edit goals to open a list of your goals right on the page. Changes save as you make them, and Add a goal is at the bottom. A Savings goal tracks how much of your monthly income is left after spending. A Spending limit tracks what you have spent this month, for all spending or one category. Earnings, Investing, and Custom goals are yours to name: you enter the amount you have so far and an optional deadline. A Checklist goal is a list of steps (one per line) that you tick off right on the goal card, and its ring shows how many steps are done.

How is goal progress calculated?

Savings and Spending goals update automatically for the current month from your recorded transactions and your monthly income (or budget if no income is set). They reset each month. For the other goals you update the amount yourself. If you add a deadline, the card shows about how much to set aside each month to finish on time.

Why does my savings goal show nothing saved?

Savings progress is your monthly income minus your spending, so it needs an income. Add your monthly income in Settings under Budget & income.

What do the colors on a goal mean?

The ring color matches the goal's category, such as green for Housing and coral for Food. For a spending limit, the text under the amounts turns gold-brown within 10% of the limit and red when you are over it.

Settings and account

What do the Preferences switches control?

Daily account sync allows automatic transaction refreshes. Budget reminders enables alerts when a category reaches 90% of its limit. Leaderboard visibility controls whether your own entry appears on the Friends page.

Do I need to save my changes?

No. The category and goal lists on Details save each change as soon as you leave the box or pick an option, and show "Saved" next to it. Press Esc in a box to undo what you typed there.

How do I change my email address?

Open Settings, choose Edit details, and save your new email. Supabase may ask you to confirm the new address through an email link before the change takes effect.

Will my settings follow me to another browser?

Mostly. Your monthly budget and income, goals, display currency, transactions, connected accounts, custom categories, merchant rules, and account preferences are saved with your Money Man account. Category limits, streaks, and a few display settings are stored only in the browser you use.

How do I change the currency amounts are shown in?

In Settings, open Currency and pick one from the list. It saves right away and applies on every page and in Ask.

Does changing the currency convert my amounts?

Yes. Everything is stored in US dollars and converted using the European Central Bank's daily exchange rate, so a figure can shift slightly from day to day as rates change. Amounts you type are converted back to dollars when saved. Bank balances show in your chosen currency, with the bank's own figure beside them.

Using Money Man

Are the chat answers generated by AI?

Yes. Ask uses an AI model from Groq. To answer, it sends your messages and saved conversation, plus recent monthly spending totals by category, your saved budgets, and your goals. It does not send individual transaction names. Answers can be wrong, so double-check important numbers, and use Clear history in Ask to delete the saved conversation. See Privacy & data for details.

How do I send feedback or report a problem?

Choose Send feedback in Settings (under More), at the bottom of Details, or at the bottom of this page. Pick what kind of feedback it is, write a few lines, and send. The team reads every message. You can tick the box if you are happy for us to email you back.

Where is Settings?

Tap the gear icon in the top right of any page. Bank accounts are not in Settings; they have their own Accounts tab in the bottom navigation.

Is the Friends leaderboard based on real friend activity?

Your friend code is tied to your account, but friend names and points on the Friends page are currently sample data. The visibility switch hides your own entry in that page; it does not publish or retrieve real rankings between accounts.

Still need help?

Email MoneyMan@MoneyMan.BYU.edu. Include the page you were using and what happened. Do not send your bank password, Plaid credentials, or full account number.
