1. Operator and contact

Money Man is a budgeting application operated by Team Money Man ("Money Man," "we," or "us"). Our mailing address is 7150 TNRB, Brigham Young University, Provo, UT 84602-3113. You can contact us at MoneyMan@MoneyMan.BYU.edu. Where privacy law uses the term, Team Money Man is the controller of the information described in this notice.

2. Information we handle

Account information

We use your email address and the first and last name on your account. The account record also includes an internal user identifier, friend code, account preferences, and account timestamps. Authentication is provided by Supabase Auth.

Financial account and transaction information

For a bank or other financial institution you connect, the app stores the institution name, Plaid account identifier, account name and type, account mask when available, cached balance, currency, and balance update time. Imported transactions can include merchant description, amount, date, category, account, and institution. Transactions you enter manually are stored with the same basic transaction details. Plaid access tokens are stored by the server in encrypted form; Money Man does not receive your bank's login password through Plaid Link.

Budget and app information

Custom categories, merchant categorization rules, manual transaction changes, monthly budget and income values, goals, and your Settings choices (daily sync, budget reminders, leaderboard visibility, and display currency) are stored with your Supabase account. Everything is stored in US dollars; your display currency only changes how amounts are shown, using daily exchange rates the server fetches from a public rate service (Frankfurter, European Central Bank data) that receives no information about you. Names you give your banks and accounts, and which accounts you hide, are stored with your account too. Category limits, streaks, and some app display settings remain in this browser, along with a cached copy of your display currency and the exchange rates.

Feedback you send

When you use Send feedback, we store your message, the type you chose, the page you sent it from, and basic browser details. If you tick the box that lets the team email you, we also store your email address with that message. The team reads feedback to improve the app, and you can ask us to delete a message by emailing us.

Chat and technical information

When you use Ask, your messages and prior saved conversation are sent to Groq to generate replies, along with recent monthly spending totals and category summaries, saved monthly budgets, and goals. Individual transaction names are not included in that spending context. Successful user and assistant messages are saved in your account's Chatbot History until you choose Clear history in Ask. Groq processes the information it receives under its own terms and privacy practices. The app also uses an essential session cookie and receives ordinary connection information needed to deliver and secure the service. When you connect a bank, your browser loads the Plaid Link script from Plaid's servers, so Plaid may receive connection information such as your IP address and browser details.

3. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account, authenticate you, and provide the app.
  • To connect accounts through Plaid, retrieve balances and transactions, and keep imported transactions in sync.
  • To organize transactions, apply your category choices and merchant rules, and show spending summaries.
  • To provide the AI chat responses you request, using your saved conversation and budgeting context.
  • To calculate progress on your savings and spending goals from your recorded transactions and monthly income.
  • To save your account preferences and apply sync, reminder, leaderboard, and currency settings.
  • To diagnose service errors, protect the service, respond to requests, and meet legal obligations.

If a privacy law requires a legal basis, the appropriate basis depends on your location and the specific processing. It may include providing a service you request, your consent where required (including for a bank connection), our legitimate interests in operating and securing the service, or compliance with law. You may withdraw consent for future processing where consent is the applicable basis.

4. Connected financial accounts

If you choose to connect an institution, Plaid provides the connection flow and transmits supported account and transaction information to Money Man. Plaid and your financial institution process information under their own terms and privacy notices. Plaid may have separate product availability or fees. Review Plaid's privacy information before connecting an account.

Disconnecting an institution removes its saved connection and account records from the app. Transactions already imported remain in your transaction history, and manual transactions are not removed by disconnecting a bank. Cached balances may be delayed and are not guaranteed to be real-time.

5. When information is shared

We use service providers to operate Money Man:

  • Supabase provides authentication and cloud database services.
  • Plaid provides bank linking and financial data services when you connect an account.
  • Groq processes chat messages and budgeting context to generate AI replies.

These providers process information under their own terms and the agreements with the service. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or the public. We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs.

Provider locations and your location may mean information is processed in countries other than where you live. Where required by law, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers of personal information.

6. Storage and retention

Account, linked-account, category, transaction, budget, goal, preference, and chat history records are stored in the operator's Supabase project. Category limits, streaks, and some app settings are stored locally in the browser. A short-lived transaction snapshot may be kept in the current browser tab to make recently viewed data load faster.

We retain information while your account is active and for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You may request account closure and deletion by contacting us. We delete or de-identify information that we no longer need, unless law requires us to retain it. Residual copies may remain in secure backups for a limited period. Imported transactions remain in your history when you disconnect a bank; disconnecting removes its saved connection and account records, not previously imported transactions.

7. Cookies and browser storage

Money Man uses an essential, HttpOnly session cookie to keep you signed in. It is configured as SameSite=Lax and is set as Secure in production; its lifetime is seven days. The app also uses browser local storage for category limits, your cached display currency, and other app settings, and session storage for temporary transaction snapshots. These technologies are used for the service rather than advertising. You can clear browser storage through your browser, though doing so may remove locally saved app information or end your session.

8. Security

The server keeps Supabase credentials out of browser code. Plaid access tokens are encrypted by the server, and database access is restricted using user-scoped queries and Supabase Row Level Security. Authentication sessions use an HttpOnly cookie. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure; the operator should maintain and periodically review appropriate technical and organizational safeguards.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws may give you rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of personal information; to object to or restrict certain processing; to withdraw consent; or to appeal a decision. Some laws also provide rights related to targeted advertising, sale or sharing, or sensitive personal information. These rights are subject to legal limits and may not apply in every case.

You can update your name, email, password, currency, and account preferences in Settings (the gear icon). You can disconnect a bank on the Accounts page and delete manual transactions on Details. To exercise other rights, email MoneyMan@MoneyMan.BYU.edu. We may ask for information to verify your identity and will respond within the time required by applicable law. You may also contact your local data protection authority or privacy regulator.

10. Children's privacy

Money Man is designed for college-age users and is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us at MoneyMan@MoneyMan.BYU.edu so we can review and address the request.

11. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as the service or applicable requirements change. The date at the top indicates when it was last revised. If a change requires notice or consent, the operator will provide it as required by law.
